Recreation

Programs

These are the current offerings. This site does not take registrations. Call the office or use the contact page if you want a paper form or a scholarship packet.

Youth Swim Lessons

Group lessons for ages 4–12 at the Civic Recreation Center pool. Sessions run three weeks.

When
June and July, weekday mornings
Where
Civic Recreation Center
Fee
$45 per session

Senior Walking Club

A staff-led walk on Mill Creek Greenway. No fitness test. Water and a bench stop at the halfway mark.

When
Tuesdays and Thursdays, 8:00 a.m.
Where
Mill Creek Greenway trailhead
Fee
Free

Summer Day Camp

Week-long camps for ages 6–12 with swimming, crafts, and field games. Lunch is provided.

When
June through early August
Where
Civic Recreation Center
Fee
$90 per week

Community Yoga in the Park

Outdoor yoga on the Bayfront Commons lawn. Bring a mat. Held in fair weather only.

When
Saturdays, 8:30 a.m., March–November
Where
Bayfront Commons
Fee
Free; donations optional

Junior Ranger Nature Series

Guided walks for ages 7–14 covering birds, pines, and inlet ecology at Whispering Pines Preserve.

When
Second Saturday of each month, 9:00 a.m.
Where
Whispering Pines Preserve
Fee
Free

Adult Tennis League

Round-robin doubles on weekday evenings. Beginner and intermediate flights. Rackets not provided.

When
April–June and September–October
Where
Civic Recreation Center courts
Fee
$30 per season
